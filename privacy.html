SunTally Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

The short version: SunTally keeps your data on your phone. Your Sunsynk sign-in details are stored only in your device's secure storage, your energy data is cached only on your device, and we operate no servers of our own. Nothing is sold, shared or tracked.

Who we are

SunTally is a companion app for owners of Sunsynk solar inverters. Contact: suntally.app@gmail.com. SunTally is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sunsynk.

What the app collects and where it goes

  • Sunsynk account credentials. Used solely to sign in to Sunsynk's service on your behalf. Stored exclusively in your device's hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore). They are transmitted only to Sunsynk's own servers, never to us or anyone else.
  • Your energy data (solar production, consumption, battery state, inverter settings). Retrieved from Sunsynk's service and cached in a database on your device so history and costs work offline. It is not transmitted anywhere else.
  • Tariff settings you enter (your electricity tariff, country, currency). Stored on your device only.

What the app does NOT do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data — we never see it.
  • No accounts on our side: we run no backend servers.

Third-party services

The app communicates with Sunsynk's cloud service (api.sunsynk.net) to retrieve your plant's data, exactly as the official Sunsynk Connect app does. Sunsynk's own privacy policy governs their processing. If weather-based forecasting is enabled in a future version, anonymous location-area requests to public weather services (such as Open-Meteo) will be described here before release.

Deleting your data

Sign out to remove your credentials from secure storage, or uninstall the app to remove everything, including the cached energy database. We hold nothing to delete on any server.

Children

SunTally is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights (POPIA / GDPR)

Because your data never leaves your device, access, correction and deletion are entirely in your hands through the app itself. Questions are welcome at the contact address above.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published at this address and noted in the app's release notes before they take effect.
